# Community research and recognition evidence

Local implementation and original synthetic fixtures only. No production Telegram transport, real forecast data feed, valuable reward or native usability result is claimed.

Tracked calls record a measurable asset/topic, metric/operator/target, benchmark/units, original source, exact UTC deadline, confidence, disclosures and resolution rule before the outcome. A user-row lock enforces three registrations per member per UTC week across guilds. Request IDs make retries idempotent. Terms are database-immutable; an explicit retained revision consumes another registration and preserves the original call and miss. Corrections append source-resolution revisions. Internal source resolution requires the declared source/time, provenance and data class; configured permitted source readers are not installed, so real outcomes remain unresolved rather than invented. Synthetic resolutions are visibly teaching-only.

One weekly bounty has immutable criteria, source requirements, deadline and recognition-only budget. Coordinators publish it; every active member can submit or challenge. Independent challenges do not require an active bounty. Copied normalized work is rejected. No forecast-registration, posting, click, capital, trade-volume or profit event grants recognition.

Two ordinary conflict-free members rotate only for a particular award. The author and disclosed conflicts are excluded. Missing members leave a visible pending state. Reassignment cycles preserve previous assignments, and a newly declared conflict prevents an old assignment from deciding, even if no replacement is available. Written source/usefulness decisions require two members. An exact 48-hour appeal holds recognition; one independent appeal uses different members and preserves all reasons. Finalization writes separate nontransferable learning-XP and research-reputation ledger entries once. Competitive season points use their separate scoring ledger. No valuable budget, token conversion or trade permission exists.

The responsive community page keeps form text after failed writes, shows allowance/criteria/budget/misses/reasons/appeal timing, and retains older calls and decisions through guild-bound six-microsecond cursors. Exact submission links provide authorized decisions and appeals without relying on the newest list page. All active members retain ordinary discussion when the forecast allowance is exhausted.

Regular Telegram `/track`, `/bounty` and `/results` use authorized bound-guild website links; there is no Mini App. A weekly digest is separately opted in and checks the exact private bot start, current identity, active guild membership and current subscription before sending. It contains selected research state, not a full chat or private financial amounts. A durable operation ID prevents resending uncertain deliveries; unsubscribed/departed recipients produce no card/send. Sending remains disabled.

Rulings:

- Three forecasts/member/week is global across guilds, following the member-based cap and preventing multi-guild posting-volume workarounds. Ordinary contributions remain open. Cost if wrong: change the explicit cap scope in a future policy revision.
- Coordinators publish the single bounty's immutable criteria; award decisions belong only to the two rotating ordinary members. This is coordination, not a research-role gate. Cost if wrong: revise bounty-publication authority while preserving participation and source/award controls.
