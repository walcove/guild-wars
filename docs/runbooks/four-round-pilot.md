# Four-round dual-mode pilot — not started

This runbook is preparation only. An explicit release decision, approved provider/data/eligibility evidence, native Telegram verification and the operational packet must pass first. Local development authorization does not authorize registration with a provider, a funded transaction, a production bot message or a hosted release.

Before entry, a named human operator registers small eligible real-trading and simulation guilds and publishes versioned mode/division/rules, four actual expirations, UTC timetable, quote/fill policy, fees, reserves, data permissions, comparison policy, recognition rules and support path. Native bot/group administration and identity-return recovery must be checked on desktop and mobile. Freeze original opening rosters and the separately consenting real cohort before the specified opening. Ordinary research/voting remains open to every active member; nobody must trade for access or recognition.

Observe comprehension with newcomers: ask them to explain the covered-call obligation, maximum sale price/capped upside, retained SOL downside, premium versus total P&L, saved vote status and their own separate provider authority. Record observed answers and recovery confusion; do not preclaim comprehension scores from screenshots or automated tests.

For each of four elapsed weekly rounds, record the exact source/quote availability and versions, precommitted House, original denominator, vote revisions/outcome, execution window and individually declined/rejected/authorized orders. Only the member/provider can authorize that member's order. Include costs, partial/unknown states, provider access incidents, live/simulation quality gaps, independent challenges, optional notifications and support load. Reconcile authoritative settlement/claim/withdrawal receipts; elapsed expiration is not receipt. A real expiry may require calendar time. Do not substitute a synthetic settled record.

Keep four evidence classes distinct: implemented/local synthetic, authorized provider sandbox, permitted authoritative records, and separately confirmed funded/native events. A sandbox path alone does not establish funded settlement or a four-week pilot. Publish privacy-safe aggregates with frozen cohort completeness, at least three verified members, five eligible guilds for official divisions and explicit provisional reasons. Losing/missing members cannot be removed.

After round four, review research-to-vote participation, retention, breadth/opposing evidence, comprehension, usable quote coverage, costs, incidents and source quality. Compare only equivalent eligible quotes; market movement alone does not establish guild skill. Publish a source-backed next-season decision under a new rules version, with human review. The MVP remains incomplete until both modes, A1–A20 and native/provider evidence pass or the user explicitly changes requirements.
